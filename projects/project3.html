VR Game Controlled by MI-BCI for Upper Limb Rehabilitation

The aim of this system is to integrate a rehabilitation environment for upper limb motor recovery in the form of a VR game with a MI-BCI. In the system, the user performs left and right arm MI, which is detected using ML algorithms to perform a movement and ability within the VR game. The left-hand MI triggers a fire ability while the right-hand MI triggers an ice ability. With these abilities the user attacks the enemies, fire beats the ice (blue) enemy and ice beats the fire (orange) enemy.

System Functionality
  • Motor Imagery Detection: Left/right arm MI detected by ML algorithms
  • VR Abilities:
    • Left MI triggers fire ability
    • Right MI triggers ice ability
  • Game Mechanics:
    • Fire defeats ice enemies
    • Ice defeats fire enemies
